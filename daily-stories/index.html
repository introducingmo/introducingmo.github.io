Why invest in daily stories?

Stories is the most underrated tool out there to get the attention of your audience. We all watch stories every day, but which of those stories are memorable? In this plan, we will use stories to help your brand stand out and pull in the right people.

  • 01

    First thing they see

    Stories sit at the top of the app, above the feed. A story every day keeps your name in the first row your followers tap.

  • 02

    Always on brand

    One look, one voice, every day. People start recognising you before they read your name.

  • 03

    No daily scramble

    I plan the month, design and edit every story, and post them if you want. You approve in WhatsApp and get on with your day.

  • 04

    Skyrocket engagement

    Polls, quizzes, questions and sliders turn viewers into tappers and repliers. And Instagram ranks your next story on exactly that: taps, likes and replies, says Adam Mosseri, Head of Instagram.

    Boost visibility

    Link stickers take people straight to your menu, your booking page or your WhatsApp, while the craving is still fresh.

Still got questions?

Is there a minimum commitment?

The monthly prices need a minimum of 4 weeks. If you’d rather not commit, pay by week: no minimum, at a higher weekly rate. You can switch to monthly whenever you’re ready.

What does “with posting” mean?

I publish the stories on your account for you, for $5 more a week. Without posting, you receive each story ready to post, and you publish it yourself.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo and any brand guidelines, plus photos and footage of your products, your space and your team. The more raw material you send, the more variety your stories get.

What’s the difference between the packages?

Stills is designed images only. Motion adds edited video stories, which is where most of the attention goes. Domination gives you up to five stories a day instead of one, so you show up from morning to night.

Do you do more than stories?

Yes. Daily stories is one service. For brand strategy, identity and launch campaigns, see my services and case studies.
