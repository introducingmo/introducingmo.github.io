Case study 03 · Endure

Agora. The building fell. The brand didn’t.

A coffee shop lost its home, more than once. Each time it reopened, the people came back.

  • ClientAgora Coffee
  • WhereSouth Lebanon to Beirut
  • Type of businessCoffee Shop
  • ScopeBrand Strategy & Community Building
Photo to comeAgora: the shop and its regulars
01

The problem

Most cafés compete on coffee, price and a nice chair. Any of those can be copied by the shop across the street.

Agora needed a reason to be chosen that a competitor couldn’t copy, and that would hold even if the coffee, the room or the street changed.

02

What I saw

People don’t stay loyal to a building. They stay loyal to who they are there.

An agora was the ancient town square, the place a community gathers. The name already held the idea. The brand’s real asset wasn’t the shop. It was the regulars, their rituals and the conversations around the tables.

03

The decision

So, for about a year, we built the brand around the community rather than the café: its people, its voice and its habits. We made the regulars part of the story, so that Agora was something they belonged to, not just somewhere they went.

04

What happened

Then the shop was lost. It reopened elsewhere in the south, bigger, in a 400 m² two-floor space, and that one was lost to the war too.

The people followed the brand each time. Today Agora is open again in Halde, closer to Beirut, and growing step by step. I’m back as its brand strategist.

That is what gravity looks like. When the walls are gone, the pull is still there.
